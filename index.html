Flappy Levels: Free Endless Flappy Bird Style Game

Flappy Levels is a free browser-based flying game inspired by classic Flappy Bird gameplay. Tap, click, or press space to flap and guide your ball through an endless series of pipes. No download, no install, no sign-up — just open the page and play instantly on desktop or mobile.

How to Play

Tap the screen, click, or press the spacebar to flap upward. Gravity pulls you down between flaps, so time your taps to glide safely through each gap. Every pipe you clear adds to your score, and the game never ends — survive as long as you can for a new high score.

Endless Themed Levels

Every 10 pipes you pass, Flappy Levels shifts into a new visual theme — Day, Sunset, Night, Space, Underwater, Desert, Cyberpunk, and Volcano — each with its own obstacle style and difficulty, then cycles endlessly so there's always a new look ahead.

Coins, Power-ups, and Achievements

Collect coins for a persistent total, grab power-ups like shields, slow-motion, and score multipliers, build combo streaks for bonus points, unlock ball skins, and complete achievements as you play. Come back daily for a bonus coin spin and streak rewards.

Flappy Levels works great on phones, tablets, and desktop browsers — play free anytime, anywhere.
